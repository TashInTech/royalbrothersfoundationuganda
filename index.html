About Us

Who we are

Royal Brothers Foundation Uganda was founded by Royal Brothers in Masaka with one belief: nobody should be left to struggle alone.

We reach out to vulnerable people, the unemployed, the homeless and others without care, giving them practical help and the dignity they deserve.

Our mission: to provide support, care and opportunity to people with no one to turn to.
Our vision: a community where no one is left behind.

Our Programs

How we help

Practical programs that meet urgent needs and build lasting independence.

Education Support

School fees, uniforms and learning materials for children who would otherwise stay at home.

Health Care

Medical check-ups, medicine and referrals for people who cannot afford treatment.

Emergency Relief

Food, blankets and basic supplies for families hit by hardship or disaster.

Counselling

A listening ear and guidance for people facing trauma, loss or despair.

Skills & Training

Hands-on training and start-up support so the unemployed can earn an income.

Shelter & Food

Safe shelter and meals for the homeless while they rebuild their lives.

Our Challenges

What stands in our way

The need is growing faster than our resources. These are the gaps we are working hard to close.

Limited funding

Most of our work depends on kind donations, which are not always steady.

Shelter shortage

Many homeless people need safe places to sleep, and we cannot house everyone yet.

Food insecurity

Rising food prices make it hard to feed all the families who come to us.

Medical costs

Treatment and medicine are expensive for people with no income.

Few volunteers

We need more hands for outreach, teaching and counselling.

Distance and transport

Reaching remote villages needs transport and fuel we often lack.
